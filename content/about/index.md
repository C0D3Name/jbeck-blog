I am Jonathan Beck, a software engineer who started working professionally in
2007. This site is where I write about what I am building, measuring, and
learning.

## What I work on

My background spans enterprise software and solutions architecture. More
recently my focus has been on **AI architecture and benchmarking** — not
demos, but the parts that matter in production: how systems are structured,
what they actually cost, and how they behave when measured honestly.

I also write about **AI-assisted development pipelines**, because the tooling
around how we build has changed more in the last two years than in the decade
before it, and most of what gets written about it is not grounded in real use.

Game development remains a hobby, and it occasionally shows up here.

## The stack

C# and .NET have always been my favorite place to work, and Azure is where I
am most at home architecturally. I currently work in Python on AWS in FinTech,
which has been genuinely useful — having depth on both sides of that divide
changes how you evaluate either one. Expect some writing that compares them
directly.

## Bitflection LLC

I have run [Bitflection LLC](https://bitflection.com) since 2018. It is the
vehicle for my contracting and consulting work, and I am currently positioning
it toward government contracting.

This site is the personal and professional side — my writing and my voice.
Bitflection is the company.
